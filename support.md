# Linden Support

Linden reads your resting heart rate from a 30-second face scan, processed
entirely on your phone. If you need a hand, this page covers the most common
questions — and how to reach a person.

## Contact us

Email **[relorbany@hotmail.com](mailto:relorbany@hotmail.com)** and we will get
back to you. Including your device model and iOS version helps us help you
faster.

## Getting a good reading

- **Light matters most.** Linden works best indoors — near a window or under
  cool, even light. Warm bedside lamps and dim rooms make the pulse signal
  harder to read.
- **Hold still.** Prop your phone against something at face level and stay
  relaxed for the full thirty seconds.
- **Keep your distance.** About an arm's length away, with your face filling
  the oval from chin to forehead.
- If a scan reads poorly, Linden tells you what to adjust and does not save it,
  so your streak and baseline are unaffected.

## Is my data private?

Yes. The video of your face is processed on your phone and then deleted — it
never leaves your device. Linden has no account system, and we never sell your
data. Full details are in our
[privacy policy](https://rumbooo0.github.io/linden-legal/).

## Managing your subscription

Linden Plus is an optional subscription billed through your Apple Account. To
view, change, or cancel it, open the **Settings** app on your iPhone, tap your
name, then **Subscriptions**, and choose Linden. Cancelling keeps everything you
have already recorded — it only pauses the Plus features.

## Deleting your data

Everything Linden stores lives on your phone. To remove all of it, delete the
app — iOS clears the local storage when you uninstall.

## A note on what Linden is

Linden is a general wellness app, not a medical device. It does not diagnose,
treat, cure, or prevent any condition, and it is not a substitute for advice
from a clinician. If you feel unwell, talk to a medical professional.
